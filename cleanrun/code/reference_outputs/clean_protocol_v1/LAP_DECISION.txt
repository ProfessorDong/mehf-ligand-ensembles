LAP arm decision — written before any clean-protocol LAP number exists
=======================================================================
Date: 2026-10-24
Decision: TRAIN MEHF-LAP under the clean protocol and read it on test.

Honest statement of when this decision was made
-----------------------------------------------
This decision was taken AFTER the clean ISO/HARM test read of 2026-10-24
(primary Delta -1.0345 [-1.263, -0.918], Tier R; secondary -0.9398, Tier A;
headline Tier A under the downgrade-only rule). It was NOT taken blind. The
pre-registration required this file precisely so that the timing could not be
misrepresented later, and the timing is therefore stated plainly here rather
than implied.

Why it is being done
--------------------
MEHF-LAP is the closest published comparator (a graph-Laplacian / harmonic
source of the EigenFold and HarmonicFlow family). Under the original protocol
it was the second-best arm (spread 1.352 against 0.770 for Sigma*). It was not
retrained for the clean protocol, so Table III's four-way comparison still rests
on the full-trajectory pocket crop while the ISO/HARM comparison does not. That
asymmetry is a defect in the paper: the method's advantage over the best
published alternative has not been shown under the corrected protocol.

What it costs
-------------
This will be the FOURTH read of this test split. Section 0 of the
pre-registration already discounts the nominal 95% intervals for three reads,
and Amendment 3 raised the comparison count to four; this read raises it again.
The LAP result must therefore be reported with that cost stated, and it may not
be used to revise or reinterpret the ISO/HARM headline, which is already fixed.

What is fixed in advance
------------------------
- Arm definition, architecture, splits, losses, 8-epoch budget, folds 1-3, the
  exclusion list of 28 systems and the train-only tau: identical to the clean
  ISO and HARM arms. Only the local source prior differs.
- Operating point: the same torsion-matching rule, primary on the fitting folds
  and secondary on the dev folds, both frozen to a hashed JSON before any LAP
  test command is constructed.
- The contrast of interest is MEHF(Sigma*) minus MEHF-LAP on the per-example
  channel spread, paired, 95% interval from 10,000 bootstrap resamples of
  held-out UniProt targets, on the same 1,406 usable examples.
- Blocking condition B4 is evaluated as written: if clean LAP is at or below
  clean Sigma*, the contribution loses to published prior art and must be
  reported as such.
- psi, realised torsion ratio, internal RMSF and full-uncropped-pocket clash
  rate are reported for LAP exactly as for the other arms.

Prediction, recorded before the read
------------------------------------
Clean LAP will land between clean ISO and clean Sigma* on the channel spread,
nearer to Sigma* than the original protocol's 1.352 suggested, because the
attenuation seen for ISO (1.973 -> 1.902) indicates the leak was mildly
flattering to the wide-ensemble arms and mildly penalising to the narrow ones,
and LAP sits between the two in internal RMSF (0.85). Point estimate 1.1 to 1.4,
and Sigma* minus LAP negative with an interval excluding zero. Confidence 0.7.
If clean LAP comes in at or below clean Sigma*, B4 fires and the paper must say
that the proposed prior does not beat the published graph-Laplacian source once
the pocket leak is removed.
